# Frame: concern-shaped shared audiences

This candidate frame covers semantic topic adoption under the [turnaround](coordination-turnaround.md), alongside [project discovery](turnaround-projects.md). Current routing remains in [messaging](../architecture/messaging.md) and [addressing guidance](../../plugin/skills/agent-bus/references/addressing.md).

Status: proposed discovery facet of the single orientation vertical, not a separate team. DRA: Codex lead; shared model and composed orientation are owned by the parent frame. Mode: co-framing; object: solution.

## What and why

A topic is the audience for a concern or workstream within a project, not every person who happens to work on that project. The real preview-delivery case involved a source owner, submitter and publisher; unrelated agents did not need every intermediate status message.

In are semantic topic discovery, deliberate subscription changes and shared status/decision flow. Foreground fewer director relay episodes without losing a necessary participant. Exterior are forced reply-all, mandatory acknowledgement, automatic task assignment and one catch-all project channel.

The cheaper alternative is better direct-message guidance. Retain direct mail for targeted assignments and follow-ups, but test whether a concern audience eliminates repeat relay that individual addressees cannot. If improved direct-message guidance yields the same real newcomer and coordination outcome without higher direct-message burden, prefer it over more routing machinery. Reusing the old project-wide example would not test this comparison.

## Hard core and assumptions

1. Topic identity names a concern and has an explicit project relationship when that concept is accepted.
2. Subscription controls delivery, not authority or a requirement that every subscriber answer.
3. The reply audience is chosen deliberately; current participants can discover the intended shared route.

Assume at least one actual multi-owner concern persists long enough for shared context to help. Topic granularity, naming, discovery and transition policy are adjustable; measure workload rather than rewarding more channels.

## Adoption and falsifier

Use the parent frame's predeclared cohort/window and mechanically countable direct-message-volume proxy, produced through saved queries after query deployment. Agree comparable workload, active concern-days and exclusions before changing instructions. Through the real coordinator, onboard one concern and update its durable instructions; current participants learn through the built-in bulletin. Consumer-judged relay examples, missing necessary recipients and unnecessary interruptions validate what the proxy means. No semantic restatement classifier is assumed. Topic share alone is not success. If actual relay does not fall or missed coordination rises, reframe the audience boundary before expanding.
