# Orientation

Use `bus_orient` with your absolute `cwd` to discover candidate directories and programme guidance. Supply `directory` explicitly to select one, and `concern` to read that concern's full bulletin. A unique candidate from a complete first page may be selected for you; ambiguity, unresolved paths, missing records, expiry and withdrawal are labelled. A directory's references and the designated bulletins are explicit guidance, not authority inferred from a recent message. They do not override your user's instructions or grant permission to act.

All returned ledger facts share the reported `cut`. `evaluatedAt` is the separate expiry-check time and `catalogueVersion` is the query code version. Other concern bodies are omitted; use their same-cut route when needed. `members` are explicit associations, not proof of activity or subscription; use the returned `peers` route to inspect subscriptions. `unresolvedAssociations` are diagnostics, not members of a missing directory or concern.

When status is `INCOMPLETE`, continue the candidate page with its `next` as `candidateCursor` and the same `cwd`, or continue the association page through `bus_run_query` using its `name`, `args`, `t` and `next`. An empty page with a continuation is not an empty complete answer. A damaged or unavailable result is not current guidance; follow its `recovery` without pretending it is empty. If a full association unit cannot fit, the error gives a new membership-projection traversal at the same cut; do not pass the full projection's cursor to it.

You may request `orient: true` on `bus_join`. The join commits first: if `orientation` is unavailable, retry `bus_orient` read-only, not `bus_join`. `associations` are an explicit array such as `[{"directory":"project","concerns":["delivery"]}]`. Omit them to preserve a same-session, same-runtime joined rejoin, including replay; `[]` clears. After a leave or takeover, provide them again if intended. Association never subscribes you to a topic. The shell CLI offers `agent-bus join --orient` and `agent-bus orient`; an association-bearing CLI join checks the fresh server's schemas before mutation. A cached client needs a fresh connection to discover new fields.

Only when commissioned to designate guidance, use `bus_directory` for a complete directory or `bus_bulletin` for a programme or registered concern. Supply your joined `from` and `session`, a fresh UUIDv7 `operation`, and `expected: null` only for an initial write; replacement names the current revision. Directory writes require an explicit nonempty `audience`. Programme notices go to `*`; concern notices go to the registered topic and deliberate direct `recipients`. Withdrawal reuses the prior audience. A successful notification is not a read receipt.

`APPLIED` means the operation witness matches and returns the original receipt, even after a later designation. `CONFLICT` means the expected designation lost; inspect the new current state and decide what to do. `IDENTITY_MISMATCH` means that operation id was already used for a different intent or publisher: stop and inspect. `UNKNOWN` might have committed. Retry only the identical action, scope, expectation, payload and audience with the SAME operation id; do not automatically rebase, generate a new id, loop or claim non-delivery.
